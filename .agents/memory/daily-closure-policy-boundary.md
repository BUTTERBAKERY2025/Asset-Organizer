---
name: Daily closure financial-policy boundary
description: Daily sales follow-up and correction must not invent financial approval policy.
---
Daily closure follow-up is distinct from performance analytics and month review. Do not interpret an approved daily snapshot as proof that every journal was included or every discrepancy settled.

**Why:** The user authorized correcting the daily-follow-up window and its routes while explicitly preserving existing permissions and avoiding a new financial policy. Draft inclusion and partial journal selection are currently allowed; silently requiring approved-only journals would change that agreement.

**How to apply:** Preserve the administrator-only cancellation of an OPEN snapshot when correcting linked journals; never partially unlink while keeping stale snapshot totals or reopen CLOSED snapshots through that route. Obtain explicit policy approval before requiring discrepancy settlement or complete-day coverage as approval prerequisites.
