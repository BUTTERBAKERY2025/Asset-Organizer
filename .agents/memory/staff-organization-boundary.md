---
name: Staff organization boundary
description: Separate headquarters account classification from departmental assignment and permission grants.
---

Headquarters staff are grouped under General Administration rather than operational employees. Keep their departments unassigned until an explicit organizational assignment is made.

**Why:** The user approved separating headquarters accounts first and deciding departments later. Account roles, job titles, permission grants, and HR free-text department values are not authorization to infer that assignment.

**How to apply:** Preserve account identities, primary branches, permissions and linked HR history during directory separation. Do not apply job-role permission templates as a side effect. Organizational visibility must still require the appropriate module and branch authority. Do not equate the headquarters branch's reused warehouse identifier with warehouse-keeper privileges.
