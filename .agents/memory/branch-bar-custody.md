---
name: Branch kitchen to bar custody
description: Why internal bar handoffs start prospectively and do not establish post-sales physical stock.
---

Keep same-site kitchen-to-bar custody separate from central-kitchen orders and driver proof. Receipt requires a different authorized employee from both the document creator and the actual dispatcher.

**Why:** This movement changes responsibility within one branch, not ownership between branches. Automatically acknowledging production would erase the independent receiver's verification.

**How to apply:** Preserve branch identity and production lot throughout reservation, dispatch and receipt. Never add another branch-level production credit for the internal receipt.

Do not initialize confirmed bar custody from historical automatic display-bar receipts.

**Why:** Those receipts could be created by completing production rather than by an independent physical count; treating them as opening stock would assert evidence we do not have. Prospective activation is intentional, not a missing backfill.

**How to apply:** Any historical opening balance needs an explicit reconciliation/count workflow. Do not broaden legacy receipt synchronization to post-activation batches.

Confirmed receipt totals are not post-sales physical availability until sales and waste consumption are explicitly reconciled.

**Why:** The delivery feature deliberately does not infer item quantities from monetary cashier journals or invent a POS-to-production product mapping.

**How to apply:** Label these totals as confirmed receipts before sales/waste, not live available bar stock. Future consumption integration must preserve product, unit and lot identity and prevent repeated deductions.
