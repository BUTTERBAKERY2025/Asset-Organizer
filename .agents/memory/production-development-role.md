name: Production and development manager scope
description: User-approved all-branch operational scope and the distinction between material inventory and asset administration.
---

The production-and-development manager is intentionally an all-branches, all-kitchens role, not a branch-assignment-restricted role.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose all branches and kitchens and requested the role only, without creating or assigning an account.

**How to apply:** Keep server branch authorization, bootstrap branch lists, and user-editor explanations consistent. Do not copy the operations-manager rule that explicit branch assignments narrow access.

Interpret its inventory scope as warehouse materials and production stock, not general fixed-asset administration or accounting exports.

**Why:** This role manages production and kitchen supply operations, not finance, HR, or system administration; the generic inventory module includes unrelated asset functions.

**How to apply:** Prefer the warehouse/material-stock permission modules and existing operational reports. Avoid broad operations, finance, or generic reports grants merely to enable product editing or production reporting.
