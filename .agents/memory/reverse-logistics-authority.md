---
name: Reverse logistics inspection authority
description: User-approved separation between recipient inspection and damaged-stock write-off.
---

The receiving entity's responsible user inspects returns and classifies them as usable, quarantined for examination, or damaged. Writing off damaged stock requires operations-manager approval; the sender cannot unilaterally return goods to usable stock.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose recipient inspection with a separate operations-manager write-off approval rather than giving the recipient unilateral disposal authority.

**How to apply:** Keep quarantined/damaged goods outside available-to-issue stock. Model physical receipt, inspection/release, and final write-off as distinct audited actions. Link returns to original received shipment lines and constrain cumulative returns to received quantities. Do not treat ordinary warehouse-to-warehouse transfers as returns without an original shipment.
